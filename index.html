English Conversation Tutor
Beginner to Advanced level English conversation with smart grammar feedback, vocabulary boost, and Urdu translation support.
📖
Quranic Arabic Tutor
Learn Classical & Quranic Arabic — not Modern Standard Arabic — covering grammar (Nahw & Sarf), Quranic vocabulary, and Islamic text comprehension. Rendered in Scheherazade Uthmani-style font.
✨
Smart Feedback Engine
Every response includes a dedicated feedback block analyzing your errors, offering corrections, and suggesting better vocabulary alternatives.
🎤
Voice Input (Speech-to-Text)
Tap the microphone to speak your message. Supports both English and Arabic voice recognition.
🔑
Multi-Key Round-Robin
Add multiple Gemini API keys — the app rotates them automatically to avoid rate limits and ensure uninterrupted sessions.
📶
Offline-Ready & Self-Contained
Single HTML file — no installation required. Works on any device with a browser. API keys stored locally.
🎓
Madaris-Focused Content
Designed for Islamic seminary students — topics include Fiqh, Hadith, Dawah, Islamic ethics, and classical Arabic scholarship.
